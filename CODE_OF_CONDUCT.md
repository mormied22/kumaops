# Community guidelines

KumaOps welcomes people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful,
assume questions are asked in good faith and help make technical discussions accessible.

Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory language,
threats, doxxing, spam and disclosure of another person's private information are not
acceptable. Keep contributions relevant and provide constructive, reproducible feedback.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions or restrict
participation. When practical, they will explain the relevant behavior and how to improve
it. Apply these guidelines consistently and respect the privacy of people involved.

Do not post sensitive details about an incident publicly. For immediate platform abuse,
use GitHub's reporting tools. For project concerns, request a private reporting channel
without including sensitive details; do not treat public issue comments as private.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
